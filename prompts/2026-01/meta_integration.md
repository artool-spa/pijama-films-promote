# Meta Integration

## Partners Campaigns

Each partner have 2 meta campigns

1. For conversions
2. Landing Page view

All campaigns should be an adset of one of those campaigns, this information should be stored in database

## Saved audiences

In this meta ad_account there 3 types of saved audiences that are relevant to promotion, you can identified them by looking at their names. The only field that matters is interests.

### Audience type


#### Base 

They starts with 'BASE + ${BASE_NAME}'

#### Movie Gender

They starts with 'GÉNEROS CINEMATOGRÁFICOS + ${GENDER_NAME}'

#### LGBT

Named "LGBT"

### Audiences treatment

All base audiences should be joined and intersect with movie gender audience, also in there is a gender named LGBT+ in the internal platform when it is selected as gender, it will be gender all and intersected with LGBT audience.
